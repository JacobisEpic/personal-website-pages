A planner for people who time-block.

TimeMaxx does not replace your notes or calendar. It gives your day a private time ledger: what you meant to do, what happened, and where your hours moved.

  • ✓Task inbox for quick capture, with scheduling into the first open Planned slot.
  • ✓Reusable schedule templates for days that follow the same structure.
  • ✓Category colors, filters, snap settings, and backup export/import controls.

Export your day into the tools you already use.

Planning Hub moves state between Tasks, the timeline, and outside tools without adding accounts, sync, or a backend.

  • ✓Copy a complete planning brief with tasks, Planned time, Done time, and current context.
  • ✓Export a Markdown daily log for notes, reflection, or sharing.
  • ✓Use portable TimeMaxx JSON for future import flows once safeguards are complete.

Follow-through becomes visible.

Execution Score summarizes how your productive Done time compared with your plan. The month view turns that daily signal into a pattern you can actually watch change.

Execution Score
Score = Productive Done - Extra Break Productive Plan x 100

Extra Break is Done Break beyond Planned Break. None, Other, and Break are excluded from productive time.

Your day stays on your iPhone.

TimeMaxx is intentionally local-first. Core timeline and task data lives on-device in SQLite, with no account system and no server required.

✓No account. Open the app and start planning. There is nothing to sign up for.
✓No cloud dependency. Your schedule stays on your device unless you export it.
✓Offline by default. Planning, logging, and reviewing your day do not need internet.
✓Portable data. Export backups, Markdown logs, and planning context when you want them.
